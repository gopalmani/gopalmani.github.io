02 / The person behind the work

Product-minded.
Systems at heart.

I’m Deepak, a software engineer at Kredivo Group, based in Bengaluru. Previously, I worked with Paytm, WinZO, and Zepto.

Across six years in the industry, I’ve worked where product moves fast and engineering decisions matter. My focus is turning that pace into lasting value: shipping useful products, making deliberate trade-offs, and building systems that hold up in production.

Outside work, I build independent products—from everyday planning to multiplayer games and AI data tools. I care about the whole experience: what someone needs, how the interface feels, and what happens when something fails.

My defaults are simple: correctness before cleverness, clear ownership, observable systems, and human approval where the stakes are high.

Away from the keyboard: strength training, architecture, philosophy, Gita studies, and long-form writing.
